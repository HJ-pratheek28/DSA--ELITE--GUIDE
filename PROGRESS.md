## Mastery criteria

A module is complete only when you can:
1. Explain the concepts without notes.
2. Identify when a technique applies and when it does not.
3. Implement its core algorithms from memory.
4. Analyse time and auxiliary-space complexity.
5. Justify correctness with a proof or invariant.
6. Solve unseen mixed problems independently.
