# Curated Problem Ladder

A selected sequence to make practice deliberate. This is a spine, not an exhaustive list. Attempt each problem independently; use editorials only after a serious attempt and record what was missing.

**Primary practice platform:** [CSES Problem Set](https://cses.fi/problemset/)  
**Supplement:** [Codeforces problemset](https://codeforces.com/problemset) and [AtCoder Beginner Contests](https://atcoder.jp/).

## 8. Contest practice

After the foundations:
- Enter Codeforces Div. 3 / Div. 4 contests when eligible and comfortable.
- Practise AtCoder Beginner Contest A–C first, then D as skills improve.
- Upsolve every problem you could not solve during the contest.
- Maintain a separate list of problems solved only after hints/editorials.

Useful links:
- [Codeforces problemset](https://codeforces.com/problemset)

## Rules for this ladder

- Do not skip the easier tasks just because they look trivial; they train implementation accuracy.
- Do not solve by memorising an editorial. Close it and re-derive the solution.
- If a problem takes too long, record the exact missing skill and return after studying it.
- Re-solve important problems after 1, 7 and 30 days.
- Keep attempted, independently solved, hinted and editorial-assisted counts separate.
